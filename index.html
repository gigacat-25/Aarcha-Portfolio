CASE STUDY 01

Rotaract District 3192 Digital Platform

Planning and launching a centralized website and event management platform supporting 50+ member clubs across the district.

50+ Clubs Supported
District-Wide Event System
Streamlined Admin Workflows

THE PROBLEM

Over 50 Rotaract clubs were managing event registrations, attendee lists, and reporting through separate spreadsheets and messaging groups. This created constant confusion, missing details, and extra manual work for club leaders.

WHO I WORKED WITH

District Executive Council, Club Presidents, Event Chairs, Finance Directors, and district members who needed simple ways to register for events and track participation.

WHAT WE NEEDED

I met with district officers and club leaders to understand their daily challenges. We identified key features needed: easy event creation, straightforward registration, reporting tools, and different access levels for different users.

HOW WE PRIORITIZED

We focused first on the most essential features required to get the platform live — event publishing, attendee sign-ups, and basic reporting — while saving non-essential ideas for future updates.

BUILDING & LAUNCHING

I worked directly with the tech team to translate user needs into clear requirements. I helped clarify how features should work from a user's perspective and kept development on track through regular progress check-ins.

RESULT & WHAT I LEARNED

The platform brought event management into one central system for 50+ clubs, reducing manual administrative work. I learned how important it is to communicate clearly between technical developers and non-technical leaders, gather feedback early, and ensure everyone is aligned on what needs to be built.

CASE STUDY 03

ImpactOne — Smart Facility Booking Platform

Engineering a user-centric facility reservation platform that eliminated scheduling conflicts and reduced admin workload by ~60%.

~60% Coordination Reduced
Real-Time Supabase Sync
Role-Based Clerk Access

PROBLEM & USER NEEDS

Students and administrators suffered from overlapping facility requests, double-booked auditoriums, and slow manual approval chains over paper forms.

SOLUTION & ARCHITECTURE

Designed a full-stack Next.js platform integrating Clerk role-based access (Student vs Admin views) and Supabase real-time database state sync.

PRODUCT DECISIONS

Prioritized instant conflict-detection during slot selection over complex approval queues, instantly cutting double-booking attempts to zero.

MEASURABLE OUTCOME

Achieved a ~60% reduction in manual coordination effort between student event leads and campus facility managers during trial deployment.
