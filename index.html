Problem · Decision · Result
■ Project 01

Designed & built end to end · Co-authored paper

StackSwipe

Peer guidance for people learning, building and changing careers in tech.

Read the paper ↗

Problem

Early-career people don't know what to learn next, can't get replies from experienced people, and find helpful networking features behind paywalls.

What I decided

No mentor/mentee labels. People match on what they can help with and what they're learning, messaging is free, and AI icebreakers are suggested but never sent automatically.

Result

About 50 campus users, and a survey showed they found mentors and real connections. Co-authored paper in IJSAT (Vol. 16, Issue 4).

■ Project 02

Rotaract District 3192 · Jul 2026 – present

District event booking

From one-by-one manual bookings to self-serve.

See it live ↗

My role

I ran requirement sessions with district officers and club leaders and turned them into prioritised user stories.

Problem

Event bookings across the district were handled manually, one at a time.

What I decided

Ship the essentials first: clubs post their own events, attendees book themselves, payments are secure, and confirmations go out automatically.

Result

50+ clubs use it to post events and sell tickets.

■ Project 03

Product Manager · Jul 2026 – present

The Scene Co

Three clients, three business metrics. Websites and digital solutions tied to one number each.

My role

I run discovery, set scope and success metrics, own the roadmap, and work with designers, developers and content creators through launch.

Problem

Clients asked for websites, but what they needed was sales, admissions and revenue.

What I decided

Tie every project to one business number. Real estate: plot details, an enquiry form, email alerts and marketing. School: a new site plus marketing. Food brand: a redesign rooted in Indian culture and design.

Result

+10% sales, +15% admissions and +10% revenue, each 2 months after launch.

I trained as an engineer, then found I liked deciding what to build even more.

With a B.E. in AI & Machine Learning, my first projects were things I built myself: full-stack web apps at Headstarter, open-source contributions, hackathon products. Then I started organising. As Lead Organizer of TEDxICEAS, I led a 40+ member team and raised ₹4 lakh+ in sponsorship. Through Rotaract, I ran community projects with real budgets and real partners.

Along the way I noticed that the part I enjoyed most wasn't writing the code. It was deciding what to build, for whom and why. Today I do that as a Product Manager at The Scene Co, with an engineer's eye on every decision.
